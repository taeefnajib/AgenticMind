# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

This handbook is a fictional sample document for testing AgenticMind's ingestion, retrieval and
evaluation features. It describes the policies of an imaginary company.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you may arrange your day
freely, provided your team can reach you for scheduled meetings. Full-time employees work 37.5 hours
per week.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays.
Part-time employees receive leave pro rata. Up to five unused days may be carried over into the next
year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.

Leave requests are submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Your manager approves
or declines the request within five working days.

### Sick leave

If you are ill, notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. Absences longer than
three consecutive working days require a doctor's note. Sick leave does not reduce annual leave.

## Expenses

Submit expense claims with receipts within 30 days of purchase. Claims older than 90 days cannot be
reimbursed.

### Travel

All business travel must be approved in advance by your manager. Book economy class for flights under
six hours; business class may be approved for longer flights. Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 180 EUR
per night in major cities and 120 EUR elsewhere.

### Equipment

Employees may claim up to 500 EUR every two years for home-office equipment such as chairs, monitors
and keyboards. Laptops are provided by IT and remain company property.

## Refunds for customers

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase if the product is unused. After 30
days, and up to 90 days, a store credit is offered instead of a refund. Digital products are
refundable only if they have not been downloaded.

## Security

Never share your password. Report suspected phishing emails to security@northwind.example within one
hour. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT immediately so they can be wiped remotely.
